Cindy Tracker: Privacy Policy

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}. Published by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}. Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. This is the policy for the iPhone app.

Cindy Tracker counts the Cindy workout from your iPhone's camera. This policy says what the app does with your information. In short: it stays on your phone.{{#strava}} The one exception is Strava, and only after you connect it.{{/strava}}

What the app uses, and where it goes

  • The camera. Each picture is read on your iPhone to find where your joints are, and is thrown away. It is never saved or sent.
  • Video you record. Only when you tap REC. It is saved to Photos, like any other video, with add-only access: the app can put a video there but cannot see your photos or videos. The app never sends it anywhere.
  • Your details and workouts. The name, photo, body weight, birth year and sex you choose to enter, and your sessions, the time of each rep, heart-rate traces and settings. They are kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone. The app has no account and no server.
  • Heart rate. If you pair a watch or chest strap, the app reads the heart rate it broadcasts over Bluetooth and keeps it with that session on your iPhone.
  • Bluetooth. Used only to find and read a heart-rate device. The app never reads your location.
  • Notifications. If you set a daily reminder, the app stores the time you chose and iOS shows a notification then.
  • Music. A track you pick from Files. The app remembers where it is on your iPhone so it can play it, and does not copy or send it.
  • Speech. The count is spoken by your iPhone's own voices. The app gives them the words to say.
{{#strava}}

Strava

If you connect your Strava account, each finished workout is sent to Strava, and only to Strava. The app asks first, and nothing is sent until you tap Strava's own Connect button. What each workout sends:

  • the score, and every set with its reps;
  • when it started, and how long it took on the clock, paused and in real time;
  • calories, if you have set a body weight;
  • your heart-rate trace, if a watch recorded one;
  • a title and a short description, such as the score and the pace.

Never the camera picture, the video or your pose. The app also receives your name from Strava, to say who is connected, and keeps it on your iPhone.

Uploads are sent over HTTPS. The connection to Strava is stored in the iOS Keychain, on this iPhone only, so it is not part of an iCloud or computer backup and does not move to a new iPhone. An upload can wait until you next open the app. Automatic upload can be turned off in the app, and DISCONNECT removes the connection from your iPhone and asks Strava to end it. What Strava does with an upload is governed by Strava's own privacy policy. To remove an activity, delete it on Strava.

{{/strava}}

What the app does not do

It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account. It does not use the advertising identifier, and it contains no third-party code that collects anything.{{#strava}} Apart from what it says to Strava, as described above, it sends nothing off your iPhone.{{/strava}}{{^strava}} It sends nothing off your iPhone.{{/strava}} It does not sell or share your information.

Backup

iCloud Backup and backups made to a computer may copy the app's data{{#strava}}, but not the connection to Strava,{{/strava}} and restore it to a new iPhone. That is a feature of iOS and of your Apple account, and you control it in Settings. Videos saved to Photos are part of your photo library, not of the app's data.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Everything stays on your iPhone until you delete it.
  • DISCARD on a session's page deletes that session, with its rep times and heart-rate trace.
  • REMOVE on the Account screen deletes your photo.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything else it stores.{{#strava}} iOS keeps Keychain items after an app is deleted, so the connection to Strava is cleared the next time the app is installed and opened; DISCONNECT removes it at once.{{/strava}}
  • Videos in Photos are yours to delete, like any other video.
  • Anything iCloud has backed up is managed in your Apple account.

Security

Your information is held in the app's private storage, which iOS keeps from other apps.{{#strava}} The connection to Strava is held in the Keychain, and uploads to Strava are sent over HTTPS.{{/strava}} The app does not add encryption of its own to the files it stores.

Children

Cindy Tracker is meant for adults. It is not directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone.
